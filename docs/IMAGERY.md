# Decorative imagery

The field PNGs in `public/images/` were generated with OpenAI's image generation tool on 1 October 2026. The solarpunk sidebar illustration was generated on 2 October 2026. These are decorative illustrations, not photographs of experiments performed by the project or evidence for the research catalog.

The field assets are preserved without retouching. The sidebar artwork is encoded as WebP without changing its dimensions or composition; the original generated PNG remains in the local generated-image archive. CSS blends and masks the images into their surrounding surfaces. The complete sidebar composition fits its width so the architecture and technology are not lost to a narrow crop. Field images use empty alternative text, fixed intrinsic dimensions, asynchronous decoding, and lazy loading; they add no words to the field buttons' accessible names. The sidebar image is a non-interactive CSS background.

Keep these assets separate from scientific sources, paper thumbnails, submitted artifacts, and provenance records. Their presence makes no claim about research activity or results.
