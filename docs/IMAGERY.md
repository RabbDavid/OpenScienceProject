# Decorative imagery

The four PNGs in `public/images/` were generated with OpenAI's image generation tool on 1 October 2026 for this interface. They are photorealistic illustrations, not photographs of experiments performed by the project or evidence for the research catalog.

| Asset                  | Subject                                                  | Use                            |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| `battery-cells.png`    | Unbranded cylindrical battery cells on an ivory tabletop | Battery longevity field        |
| `solar-panels.png`     | Photovoltaic panels in soft natural sunlight             | Clean energy field             |
| `material-samples.png` | Metal and crystal specimens on a laboratory bench        | Research reproducibility field |
| `forest-mist.png`      | Mist and pine trees in deep green light                  | Sidebar atmosphere             |

The original assets are preserved without retouching. CSS blends and masks the images into their surrounding surfaces. Field images use empty alternative text, fixed intrinsic dimensions, asynchronous decoding, and lazy loading; they add no words to the field buttons' accessible names. The sidebar image is a non-interactive CSS background.

Keep these assets separate from scientific sources, paper thumbnails, submitted artifacts, and provenance records. Their presence makes no claim about research activity or results.
